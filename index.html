About Us

Painting contractor focused on prep, product choice, and a clean handover

Krugersdorp Painters takes on the jobs owner-occupiers, landlords, and trustees actually need: full interior repaints, exterior refreshers, stairwells and common areas, crack filling before coating, ceiling touch-ups, and roof paint where it fits the scope. We work methodically so you get even colour, sharp edges, and coatings suited to each surface.

what we do

Residential repaints, small commercial jobs, and sectional-title maintenance, all under one painting contractor

We are set up for the work a professional painting contractor handles week to week: single-family interiors, townhouse exteriors, shop fronts, classrooms, and body-corporate batches. Neat execution on site

  • Roof and high-access work quoted only when it is safe and practical for our setup.
