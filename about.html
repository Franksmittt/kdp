About Us

Technical property maintenance, owner-managed on every site

Krugersdorp Painters is built around prep-first painting, roof restoration, waterproofing, and general maintenance, with a strong focus on body corporates, sectional-title schemes, and residential complexes across the West Rand, alongside private homes and commercial work. Rico runs projects personally so communication stays direct and the finish matches the specification.

Commitment to quality

We do not skip sanding, priming, or substrate repairs when the job demands it.

Systems for Gauteng weather

UV-rated exteriors, roof heat reflectance, membranes, and trade coatings matched to each substrate.

  • Phased common-area work coordinated with trustees and managing agents.
  • Quotes that separate prep, materials, and labour so scopes stay transparent.
  • Walk-through handovers: snags sorted before we leave site.
