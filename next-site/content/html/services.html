what we do

Painting, roofs, waterproofing, and maintenance for complexes, body corporates, and private clients

We are set up for phased common-area work, trustee sign-off, and clear scopes on sectional-title schemes, as well as standalone houses and commercial facades. You get the same technical prep on every job: washing, stripping failed coats where needed, primers that match the substrate, and topcoats rated for Gauteng UV and temperature swings.

Prep that earns its place

Crack repair, high-pressure cleaning, and high-build primers before colour goes on, especially on West Rand exteriors.

  • We do not paint over active damp or failed waterproofing without treating the source.

Neat sites, owner on the tools

Rico runs the work personally: no middlemen, clear communication with managing agents and trustees where applicable.

  • Stairwells, passages, parking zones, and facades coordinated to minimise disruption.
